# 3-minute demo script

1. **Home.** Day 6, ₹8,500 cash, ₹6,500 free, about ₹180 safe to spend per day. Point at the ring: green days ahead, the dip near the Day 12 mess fee.
2. **Log a spend.** Log ₹250 for food. The allowance, forecast and health change instantly.
3. **Decide.** Try a ₹2,000 purchase today. Open the three futures: buying now and waiting end level, skipping keeps ₹3,000 of headroom.
4. **Stress.** Switch on *Exam week*. The buy-now future goes Risky, with a buffer breach on Day 20 and savings touched from Day 26.
5. **Smart fix.** Back on Decide, the breaking point drops to ₹500. Apply a proven fix such as cancelling a subscription and trimming daily spend.
6. **Regret check.** Read the Future You letter, pick a mood, then skip it. The journal records an avoided impulse buy.
7. **Plan.** Add a ₹1,500 concert goal, move ₹500 into it, then run the subscription autopsy.
8. **Settle the day.** On Home, tap *Settle Day 6 and move on* and watch the month advance.
